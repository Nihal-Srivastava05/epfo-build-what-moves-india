The EPFO / PF cluster — our actual competition

Fourteen teams (including us) picked the same portal to rebuild. Every card below is graded from a first-look pass of that team's own live prototype — its copy, its stated feature set, its tone — on three axes, each out of 5.

Depth — how much of the real EPFO workflow it covers.   Clarity — how legible the UI/copy is to a non-technical member.   Distinctiveness — whether it has an idea nobody else in the cluster has. This is a text-level read of each site, not a full interactive test — treat it as a starting map, not a final verdict.
