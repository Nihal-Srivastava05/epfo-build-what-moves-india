# One-minute demo script

Narration is ~140 words, which lands near 60 seconds at a natural pace. Five
beats. If you run long, trim the landing rather than the passbook.

---

## 0:00–0:08 — Landing (`/`)

> "EPFO's services used to live across nine separate portals. This is all of them
> as one account."

*Cursor drifts over the three persona cards.*

---

## 0:08–0:18 — Member home (`/member`)

> "You sign in and get your balance, plus anything that actually needs you.
> June's contribution was never filed — so it's flagged, with the exact amount
> missing."

*Balance card, then the "One month is missing — Jun 2026" card.*

---

## 0:18–0:38 — Passbook (`/member/passbook`) — the money shot

> "A whole career folds into financial years. And the interest line — normally
> one unexplained lump sum — opens up: the rate, every month's closing balance,
> and arithmetic that adds to exactly the figure credited."

*Expand FY 2025-26, then click the interest row to open the working.*

This is the strongest twenty seconds in the demo: it's the one moment the app
visibly does something the real portal does not. Give it the time.

---

## 0:38–0:50 — Claims (`/member/claims`)

> "Withdrawals start from the life event, not a form number. Each one shows what
> you can take, and what share of your savings that actually is."

*Hover a card to reveal the Withdraw tag, then click "Pay for medical treatment"
— it lands straight on the amount step.*

---

## 0:50–1:00 — Close

> "Anything you can't find is one search away, from any screen. One sign-in, one
> record."

*Type `EPFiGMS` into the top-bar search — Help surfaces under its old portal
name.*

---

## Before you hit record

- [ ] **Record at desktop width.** The search bar is hidden below the `sm`
      breakpoint, so the closing beat does not exist on a narrow window.
- [ ] **Sign in beforehand.** OTP is `284116` (there is a copy button next to
      it). Starting signed-in saves ~8 seconds you do not have.
- [ ] **Reset demo data** — Settings → reset. This restores the missing June
      month and the KYC flags that the home-screen beat depends on.
- [ ] **Do not try to complete the withdrawal.** Continue is deliberately
      disabled while the bank IFSC issue is outstanding. Stop at the amount
      step — that is where the percentages are anyway.

## If you get a second take

Beats worth swapping in, in rough order of strength:

1. **Service history** (`/member/service-history`) — three employers, three
   member IDs, one continuous record on a single timeline.
2. **Government notices** — the same published list on the public front page and
   the signed-in home.
3. **Grievance** (`/grievance/new`) — raise one and watch the escalation ladder
   climb on its own, with the date it does so.
